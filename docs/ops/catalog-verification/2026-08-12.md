### Scope and decision rule

The mechanical result above was followed by a claim-level review of all 66 rows. A
successful request was not treated as proof of a card's facts. `verifiedAt` moved to
`2026-08-12` only when the current primary source supported the published summary,
contact path, eligibility/routing fields, and action guidance, or after the card was
corrected to match that source.

The review did not call any phone number, submit any form, or obtain private provider
records. Published phone routing was checked against the source but was not call-tested.
Exact local service areas that a provider did not publish remain owner/provider checks.
The matcher treats age maxima as inclusive, so decimal-year and month bounds were set to
include the full published age and exclude the next birthday.

Final date distribution: 60 rows are source-reviewed at `2026-08-12`; four unresolved
human-review rows remain at `2026-08-05`; CKRH and the blocked DSACK source remain at
