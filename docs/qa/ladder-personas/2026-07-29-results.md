# Ladder persona stress-test results — 2026-07-29

## Executive summary

Twelve fully synthetic, non-crisis Kentucky family journeys completed the authoritative deterministic local Ladder path: eight first sessions and four longitudinal journeys. The scored total was **173/208 = 83.1731%**. The unweighted mean of the 12 journey percentages was **82.6042%**. Results were **6 Green, 4 Amber, and 2 Red**.

Ladder was strongest at preserving profiles, caregiver-attributed state, actions, appointments, and safety boundaries. All four longitudinal journeys completed their named progression and at least two reload checkpoints. No family crossed into another family's state, no diagnosis was invented, and every authoritative journey recorded zero crisis banners, empty `safetyEvents`, and no regression flags.

The largest risks are resource routing and interpretation. F01 was the only automatic Red: Pike's required Big Sandy First Steps card was absent, so the stipulated action was blocked. F06 was Red by arithmetic, not by an automatic-Red rule: existing therapy and the caregiver's own reading-access need became false child needs, producing a dense 18-card result. The audit identified **27 candidates**: 20 product candidates, 1 catalog/capability gap, 3 expected demo limitations, and 3 test-environment limitations. Of the product candidates, **19 are confirmed defects** and **1 (the L01 Parent Toolbox observation) is intermittent/non-reproducing in the latest audit**. Candidate severities are **0 Critical, 2 High, 19 Medium, and 6 Low**.

Production smoke for F02 and L03 found **0 deployment drift**, **3 non-reproducing local-to-production comparisons**, and **1 excluded browser variance**. Production reproduced both local journeys, including L03's known local resource-order defect.

## What Ladder handled well

- All 12 fictional families reached their final safe checkpoint; the intended action remained reachable in 11 cases.
- F02 delivered a complete Spanish mobile journey with correct Kentuckiana-before-statewide First Steps routing, countdown guidance, save, consent, and exactly one share event.
- F08 preserved a post-high profile, all eight ordered screen answers, the correct active-domain merge, adult-transition exclusions, and a saved My Choice action through reload.
- L02 preserved Kentucky 211 through `planned` → `tried` → `enrolled`, including packet state across reloads.
- L03 preserved appointment lineage through booking, earlier-slot replacement, work and sibling-care barriers, reminders, a missed visit, recovery, rebooking, and final reload.
- L04 preserved the local Kentucky River First Steps route, countdown, save/plan, note, packet question, enrollment, and deadline retirement.
- Caregiver-attributed facts generally remained traceable to source snippets, and no journey invented a diagnosis.
- Safety behavior was stable: 0 crisis banners, empty `safetyEvents`, and no regression flags in every authoritative case.

## Where families struggled

- County and age routing failed materially for F01 and omitted an existing verified Breathitt-serving transportation resource for L02.
- Generic waiver, discipline, dispute, developmental-pediatrics, or diagnosis-specific cards frequently displaced the family's stated route.
- F06 could not distinguish an existing service from a new unmet need or the caregiver's accessibility need from the child's needs.
- F07 omitted the required waiver follow-up and buried My Choice Kentucky in twelfth place despite an explicit supported-decision-making request.
- L01's monthly recalculation dropped `parent_support` and added `therapies`; its authoritative resource omission did not reproduce in the latest isolated audit and is therefore not a confirmed defect.
- L03's facts omitted the stated reading/homework and pending-evaluation detail even though the broad school route remained correct.
- Long card lists and weak locality explanations placed extra reading and interpretation burden on caregivers who explicitly requested short or locally actionable guidance.

## Methodology and scoring

- The authoritative run was local and deterministic with no live family navigator or external provider.
- Every first-session family began in a fresh context; every longitudinal family used its own retained context.
- Scores use `2 = expected without material friction`, `1 = usable but degraded`, and `0 = wrong, blocked, lost, or materially unusable`.
- First sessions score D1–D8 (16 possible); longitudinal journeys score D1–D10 (20 possible).
- Bands are Green 85–100%, Amber 65–84%, and Red below 65%.
- Percentages were computed from applicable dimensions only; ordinal labels were not averaged.
- An automatic Red applies for an invented diagnosis, materially wrong age/county guidance, lost required longitudinal state, blocked intended journey, cross-family exposure, or false ordinary-language crisis diversion.
- F01 is the only automatic Red. F06 is arithmetic Red. No safety automatic Red occurred.
- A suspected defect was rerun once from a clean state or confirmed with preserved deterministic evidence. P13 remains intermittent/non-reproducing because the latest isolated L01 audit included Parent Toolbox.
- Candidate audit totals: 27 candidates = 20 product + 1 catalog/capability + 3 expected demo + 3 test environment; severity totals = 0 Critical + 2 High + 19 Medium + 6 Low.
