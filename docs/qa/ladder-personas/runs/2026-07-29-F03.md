- **Confirmed product finding:** `michelle_p_waiver` and `child_waiver` lead the therapy-only, no-diagnosis list before OCSHCN and UK Developmental Pediatrics. - **Confirmed catalog/capability gap — Medium:** diagnosis education was absent because the deterministic no-key extractor did not emit `diagnosis_education`. It is classified as a `catalog/capability gap`, not a product defect; it did not license an autism claim or an unsupported domain.
- **Non-reproducing harness issue, excluded from scoring:** the first automation draft compared the visible `Ages 0–21` label to an obsolete expected phrase. Source/DOM audit confirmed the card detail selector and all displayed first-four age bands include age four; the assertion was corrected without a third product run. An earlier detail-panel API mismatch was also corrected before the valid run and is not a product result.
