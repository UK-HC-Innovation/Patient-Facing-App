# Food Compass Live Lens — Deterministic Scores, a Live Camera Compass, and a Standalone Demo

> Built from the 2026-08-18 meeting with W. Scott Black. His "One Good Choice" prototype (https://rag.ai.uky.edu/one-good-choice/, built by the now-dissolved Center for Applied AI) scores foods 1–100 on Tufts' Food Compass and suggests healthier swaps, but stalled because scores were **inconsistent at the extremes**. The root causes are known and none is mysterious: (1) three math bugs in the original calculator spreadsheet (log10 where the cut-points are calibrated in natural log; swapped potassium:sodium cut-points; a copy-pasted fiber:carb cut-point; no clipping), (2) the CAAI backend **estimated nutrients with a neural net** (arXiv 2512.11836 — median R² 0.81, but 0.02 for flavonoids) and computed the score from the estimates, (3) foods under 5 kcal/100 g (water) are **outside Food Compass's domain by definition** and were run through the formula anyway, and (4) Food Compass pins the top/bottom ~5% of foods to exactly 100/1 by design (in the published table: 470 foods at 1, 416 at 100). This spec rebuilds the capability inside this app on the principle the codebase already enforces everywhere: **the model never computes numbers.** Scores come from a published lookup table or a deterministic TypeScript engine; the LLM only identifies what food it is looking at.

**Status:** Authored 2026-08-18; **adversarially reviewed the same day** by three verification passes (code-anchor audit against `master`, constant-by-constant audit against the supplement PDFs, cold-implementer red team). Every constant below was verified against Table S9/S10 of the FC 2.0 supplement with page citations; every code anchor was verified against the repo; the remaining genuine ambiguities are in the Discrepancy ledger with an empirical resolution procedure. Line numbers drift — re-locate by the quoted code.

## Owner decisions (locked — do not re-litigate)

1. **One scoring engine, two surfaces**: shared domain engine, surfaced in `/food` (integrated with flags/meal log) AND on a new standalone `/compass` page (shareable demo for Scott Black / UK Innovate, no patient chrome).
2. **Continuous live camera scoring** with a viewfinder overlay, plus **conversational voice** ("this is what I have…") reusing the existing realtime-voice infrastructure. On-demand paths (barcode, typed) also work; the live vision loop is throttled and passcode-gated.
3. **Alternatives included** — deterministic, from the published score table (same food group, better score), with recipe links as plain Google searches (that is all the original did). Include the original's two preference toggles (higher score / lower calorie density).
4. **No demo date; deploy to the existing Vercel project** (`patient-centered.vercel.app`) at the end.
5. Score presentation: FCS 2.0 number + band + **calorie density** (not redundant — FCS deliberately ignores energy density), hard-coded carve-outs for water/zero-cal, an "estimate" badge on partial-data (barcode/label) scores, personalization stays in the existing condition-lens flag layer — the score itself is never bent per-patient.

## The algorithm — Food Compass Score 2.0 (verified against Table S9/S10; printed-page cites)

**Reference unit: per 100 kcal.** **Not scoreable (T3, checked first):** products providing **<5 kcal per 100 g** (verbatim exclusion, Supp. Methods 1 p. 19: "We excluded infant formula, baby foods, specialized dietary foods, alcohol, and products providing <5 kcal per 100g"), plus alcohol, infant formula, baby foods, specialized dietary foods. Score range 1–100. Bands ≤30 minimize (red) / 31–69 moderate (amber) / ≥70 encourage (green) — from the FC 1.0 paper (the supplement doesn't restate them).

Core scaling: `S(v, l, h, pmin, pmax) = pmin + (pmax − pmin) × (clip(v, l, h) − l) / (h − l)`. **Always clip before scaling.**

**D1 — Nutrient ratios** (mean of the ratios whose gates pass; a gated-out ratio is absent from the mean):
| Ratio | Gate (footnote ‡) | **ln** cut-points | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unsaturated:Saturated fat (g/g; unsat = mono+poly, fallback `max(0, totalFat − satFat − (trans ?? 0))`) | ≥10% energy from fat | −0.66 → 1.77 | dairy half-weight — see ledger #3 |
| Fiber:Total carbohydrate (g/g) | ≥10% energy from carbohydrate | −7.02 → −0.78 | |
| Potassium:Sodium (mg/mg) | K ≥10 mg AND Na ≥10 mg per 100 kcal | −2.02 → 3.30 | |

Natural log. (The supplement says only "(log)"; ln is established by the CAAI writeup's explicit `ln()` and by numeric plausibility — e^1.77≈5.9 is a sane 95th-pctl UFA:SFA ratio, 10^1.77≈59 is absurd.) Canonical tripwire fixture: **UFA:SFA = 2.32, non-dairy → ln(2.32)=0.842 → S = −10 + 20×(0.842+0.66)/2.43 = +2.4; the log10 bug yields −1.3 for the same input.** Also fixture the K:Na direction (raspberries ≈ K 151/Na 1 mg per 100 g must beat cheddar ≈ K 77/Na 654).

**D2 — Vitamins** (0..+10 each at 25% RDA/AI per 100 kcal; domain = mean of **top 5 by |score|**): A RAE 225 µg; B1 0.3 mg; B2 0.325 mg; B3 4 mg; B6 0.325 mg; Folate DFE 100 µg; B12 0.6 µg; C 22.5 mg; D 3.75 µg; E 3.75 mg; K 30 µg; Choline 137.5 mg. (Verified p. 27.)

**D3 — Minerals** (top 5 by |score|): Ca 250 mg; P 175 mg; Mg 105 mg; Fe 4.5 mg; Zn 2.75 mg; Cu 0.225 mg; Se 13.75 µg; K 1175 mg (0..+10); **Na 0..−10 at 575 mg**. Iodine (37.5 µg) is defined but excluded — "Iodine and trans fats attributes were missing across the entire FNDDS 2001-2018 database, and so were excluded from the scoring" (p. 25 verbatim). We exclude it too. (Verified pp. 27-28.)

**D4 — Food-based ingredients** (**SUM**; FPED cup/oz-eq per 100 kcal; verified p. 28): Fruits 1.75 c; Dried fruits 0.75 c; Non-starchy veg 4.77 c; Dried non-starchy veg 4.18 c; Beans/legumes 0.50 c; Whole grains 1.12 oz; Nuts/seeds 1.35 oz; Seafood 3.86 oz; Yogurt 0.81 c; Plant oils 11.31 g (each 0..+10). Refined carbohydrates −10 at **1.36 oz** (ledger #4: settled, the CAAI 1.38 is wrong; footnote: refined carbs = refined grains + added sugars); Red/processed meat −10 at 2.69 oz. *(No FPED data for label foods — T2 omits D4.)*

**D5 — Additives**: Added sugar stepwise by % kcal (verified verbatim): 0%→0; <2.5%→−1; <5%→−2; <10%→−3; <15%→−4; <20%→−5; <30%→−6; <40%→−7; <50%→−8; <60%→−9; ≥60%→−10. Nitrites (proxy: % kcal from processed/cured meat) linear 0→−10 at **25% or 50% — the supplement contradicts itself, ledger #1**. Five binaries, −1 each when present in the ingredient list ("maximum contribution of −5"): artificial sweeteners/flavors/colors; partially hydrogenated oils; interesterified/hydrogenated oils; HFCS; MSG. Domain = mean(sugar, nitrite) + Σ binaries. ⚠️ The CAAI writeup **omits the binaries entirely** — do not "correct" the engine toward CAAI. Note: the published S5 scores applied binaries only to n=321 brand-matched items (p. 27 footnote) — see F8's publication-parity mode.

**D6 — Processing**: NOVA 1→+10, 2→+7.5, 3→+5, 4→−10 (verified footnote €; mixed dishes = energy-weighted non-integer class, linearly interpolated — ~2/3 of foods). Fermentation (half): % kcal from yogurt+cheese, 0→+10 at 50%; keyword-fermented foods (kefir, kombucha, injera, dosa, natto, miso, kimchi…) get +10. Frying (half): −10 if fried (by name/cook description). D6 = (S_NOVA + 0.5·S_ferm + 0.5·S_fry)/2.

**D7 — Specific lipids** (top 3 by |score|, weighted; domain ×0.5): EPA+DHA +10 at **62.5 mg** (weight 1.0 — FNDDS fatty acids are in grams; convert once, test it); Trans −10 at 30% kcal (1.0 — excluded from the published scores, see F8); ALA +10 at 0.4 g (0.5); MCFA (8:0+10:0+12:0) +10 at 0.32 g (0.5); Cholesterol −10 at 75 mg (0.5). Exact top-3 weighted aggregation: ledger #5.

**D8 — Fiber & protein**: fiber +10 at 9.5 g; protein +10 at 14 g; D8 = (S_fiber + 0.5·S_protein)/1.5.

**D9 — Phytochemicals** (×0.5): flavonoids +10 at 23.53 mg; carotenoids +10 at 8,746.81 µg; D9 = 0.5 × mean.

**Final** (verified verbatim, p. 29 footnote *): raw = Σ of the 9 domain values (D7, D9 carry their 0.5 factors). Truncate raw to **(−12.1, 35.0)** — the 5th/95th percentiles across all 58,618 FNDDS 2001–2018 item-rows — then **FCS = 100 − ((35.0 − raw)/47.1) × 99**, rounded to integer, floor 1 / ceiling 100. **These constants are pinned. Do not refit them** (a refit against a partial-domain recompute would bake the missing-domain bias into the constants).

### Discrepancy ledger (each has a resolution procedure; record outcomes in code comments with the quotes)

1. **Nitrite limit — the supplement contradicts itself.** Table S10 row (p. 28 printed): "Nitrites, % calories from processed meats −10 to 0 **25%** 0". Footnote ¶ (p. 29 printed): "**50%** energy from processed/cured meat was assigned the target low score (−10), with linear scaling down to 0%". CAAI implements 50 (following the footnote). Resolve empirically in P1: recompute processed-meat-heavy S5 rows (e.g. bacon `22601000`/`22600100`, published FCS 2.0 = 26) under both slopes; ship whichever matches, with both quotes in the comment.
2. **Final scaling — settled**: (−12.1, 35.0)/47.1 verbatim from the supplement. CAAI's (−12.43, 29.94)/42.37 is a deviation; do not use; do not least-squares refit.
3. **Dairy half-weight semantics.** "Dairy products (yogurt, cheeses, milk, and dairy fats) are given half weight for the scoring of the Unsaturated:saturated fat ratio" admits two readings: (a) halve the UFA:SFA *score* then plain-average (CAAI's reading — but CAAI is the *source* of that reading, not independent confirmation), or (b) weight 0.5 in a weighted domain mean (denominator 2.5). Resolve empirically in P1 against dairy S5 rows (cheddar/yogurt/whole-milk codes); encode the winner as a fixture.
4. **Refined-carb target — settled**: 1.36 oz (supplement cell); CAAI's 1.38 is wrong.
5. **D7 top-3 aggregation** ("weighted average of the top 3 by |score|"): weighted mean = Σ(wᵢsᵢ)/Σwᵢ vs weighted sum — resolve from the S10 Specific Lipids footnote wording in P1 and encode as a fixture (a lipid-heavy S5 row like salmon discriminates).
6. **FCS 1.0 NOVA points** (−10/−2/−1/0) appear in older tables — this build is 2.0 only (+10/+7.5/+5/−10). Never mix.

### Coverage tiers

| Tier | Input | Domains | Presentation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1 lookup** | FNDDS match (typed/vision) | ALL — published FCS 2.0 straight from Table S5, zero computation | plain score, no estimate badge; nutrient panel only for the ~2/3 of S5 foods joinable to FNDDS 2017-18 (no-panel fallback UI required) |
| **T2 label** | Barcode → OFF/FDC facts (+ ingredient text where present) | D1 (all 3 when gates pass, using the F1b extended fields), D3 partial (Na always; Ca/Fe/K when present), D5 (sugar step + binaries + cured-meat keyword nitrite proxy), D6 via the conservative UPF detector only, D7 partial (cholesterol, trans when present), D8 full | score + **"estimate"** badge; missing-domain disclosure in details |
| **T3 not scoreable** | <5 kcal/100 g, alcohol, baby/specialized foods, OR `calories === 0` | none | carve-out copy, no number ("Water is the best choice there is — it's outside this score's range.") |

T3/calorie-density fallback when serving grams are unknowable (T2 foods whose `servingGrams` is null and basis is per-serving): skip the <5 kcal/100 g check **except** `calories === 0` (still T3), and render calorie density as "unknown" — never a guessed band.

T2 UPF detector (conservative): ≥1 binary additive hit OR an industrial-marker keyword (maltodextrin, protein isolate, "natural flavor", HFCS, hydrogenated, emulsifier E-numbers…) ⇒ NOVA 4 (S_NOVA = −10, D6 = −5); otherwise omit D6. **Gate: the P1 harness must show the detector reduces T2 error on packaged-food groups; if it doesn't, ship with D6 omitted and record the numbers.** Missing domains contribute 0 to the raw sum; the harness quantifies the per-group bias and the estimate-badge copy quotes the measured MAE, not vibes.
