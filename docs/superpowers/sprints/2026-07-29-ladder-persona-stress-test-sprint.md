## Phase 1 — Discovery
**Status:** done
**Context:** Ladder is implemented at `/ladder` as a Kentucky pediatric family navigator and waitlist companion. Existing coverage includes Scott and Perry County paths, English and Spanish, screening tools, resource ranking, safety escalation, referral booking, journaling, and persistence.
**Constraints:** Personas must be realistic, non-stereotyped, safety-aware, and cover varied caregiver literacy, Kentucky counties, access barriers, and child needs.
**Decisions:** Deliverable is a full run: create a reusable synthetic family cohort, exercise every case through Ladder, and report observed failures and recommendations. Cohort size is 12 synthetic families. This first cohort excludes acute safety and crisis cases.
